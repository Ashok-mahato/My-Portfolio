01 — About

Steady hands.
Clean systems.

I look after the technology behind a pharmaceutical operation — where uptime and accuracy aren't optional, because production lines, records, and reporting all depend on systems running quietly and correctly.

Day to day that means supporting infrastructure, troubleshooting for the teams on the plant floor, and building small internal tools that remove slow, error-prone manual steps.

I'm drawn to the overlap between IT operations and practical software: a well-placed script or a simple web form can save a team hours of repetition. I'm deepening my work in web development, SQL, and automation so I can build more of those tools myself.
